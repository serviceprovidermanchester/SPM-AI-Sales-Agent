# Product roadmap — proposed work, not yet implemented

## Phase 1 — demo complete (v0.1.0)
Single-admin lead intake, scoring, pipeline, follow-ups, notes, CSV, template drafts, optional AI drafts, tests, docs, responsive UI.

## Phase 2 — deployable team CRM
Multi-user login, password hashing, MFA, role-based access, audit log, database migrations, PostgreSQL, scheduled backups, monitoring and notifications.

## Phase 3 — marketing automation
Verified email provider integration, compliant consent ledger, outbound email templates, unsubscribe controls, follow-up scheduler and delivery/webhook statuses. WhatsApp Business Platform integration only with approved templates and user opt-in.

## Phase 4 — connected business intelligence
Meta Ads and Google Ads reporting, calendar integration, landing-page tracking, CRM connectors, deal value/forecast, segment analytics and attribution.

## Phase 5 — advanced AI
Retrieval-augmented product knowledge, multilingual outreach suggestions, model evaluation, human-approval workflow, duplicate detection and configurable scoring.

## Prioritization principle
Ship security and consent before automated outbound integrations; only advertise features that can be demonstrated and tested.
